# Shorts uploader

A **local desktop tool** that turns long YouTube videos into vertical Shorts and uploads
them to your own YouTube channel and your TikTok drafts.

Shorts uploader runs on your computer. It has no hosted service, no account with the
author, and no telemetry. It uses your own API credentials, stores its history and tokens
locally, and talks only to the platforms you connect: YouTube, TikTok, and the AI provider
you configure.

## What it does

1. Downloads the YouTube video you point it at and transcribes the speech.
2. Finds the moments worth clipping and reframes them to 9:16 with subtitles.
3. Renders the finished Short locally with FFmpeg.
4. Uploads it to your YouTube channel and/or your TikTok drafts, on your instruction.
